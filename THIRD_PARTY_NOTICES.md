# Third-party notices — piterfly 1.0.0

Modified from SLOOP 2.8.5 (`bac9c05ea7627449405aa10e48da21d75b6c8c56`),
based on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments.
SLOOP upstream: <https://github.com/isod89/sloop-fm1>.
Changes include 24 patterns, 128 song entries, MIDI THRU, live granular inserts,
navigation and piterfly branding. Version/build/licensing preparation changes
dated 2026-11-08 are identified in the source manifest; inherited notices remain.

piterfly arcade artwork is GPL-3.0-only by the owner’s choice on 2026-11-08;
see `assets/piterfly/LICENSE.txt`.

This index does not replace the full licence texts. The active 86-icon device atlas is original piterfly geometry under GPL-3.0-only
(`tools/gen_pixel_icons.py`, `assets/icons-LICENSE.txt`). The restriction on
the older atlas and inherited screenshots remains unresolved; those materials and
the inherited Git history are excluded from this distribution tree. See PROVENANCE.md.
