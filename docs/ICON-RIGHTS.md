# piterfly device icons and excluded artwork

Distribution export prepared locally on 2026-11-08 from private main
`8d9ac74c4a8b7cac356a850755714dffe0149a87`; see [provenance](../PROVENANCE.md).

## Included atlas: original GPL-3.0-only geometry

All 86 device icons in `assets/icons.png` are generated by `tools/gen_pixel_icons.py`.
No pixels from the inherited Felucca atlas are loaded by that generator. The
replacement was committed as `27cc86f` before the origin main snapshot.

- Owner-selected GPL-3.0-only; full notice: `assets/icons-LICENSE.txt`.
- Preferred editable source: `tools/gen_pixel_icons.py`; regenerate with Pillow:
  `python tools/gen_pixel_icons.py assets/icons.png`.
- `assets/icons.json` retains all 86 IDs, 12×12 cells and the 3096-byte packed format.
- `tests/pixel_icons_test.py` verifies reproduction, coverage, IDs and packing.
- Fukiai in the web editor is separate MIT material with its notices retained.

## Excluded historical material

The older atlas (Git blob `e1ae8acc572102bc8179a317b5b08f8658a6503b`, SHA-256
`ec703d9bacef2d88656a7a439f6a6e2ab904cd7f245a004c77586d979defb944`), inherited
SLOOP logos/screenshots, pre-replacement proposals and the inherited Git history
are not included. `PUBLIC_EXPORT.json` lists the exact excluded files and hashes.
The historical reservation and conditional section 7 permission remain verbatim
in `LICENSES/Felucca-legacy-notice.txt`; replacement does not relicense old assets.

Current piterfly screenshots use the replacement atlas. The two comparison
images named `layout-*-before.png` are explicitly labelled schematic reconstructions
of the legacy step layout, rendered from included source with the new atlas.
They are not historical screenshots and do not load old firmware or artwork.

No author contact was made and no permission for omitted material is asserted.
The dated audit stays in the private repository. Primary evidence links are
retained in PROVENANCE.md, including the old-atlas removal and the later CC0
wave-generator dedication. Current build sources and notices remain included.
